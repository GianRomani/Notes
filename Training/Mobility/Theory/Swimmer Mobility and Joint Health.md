> **Status:** Coming soon — initial structure and study roadmap.

Anatomical and practical study of mobility versus stability for swimmers and lifters, focusing on long-term joint health and pain-free overhead mechanics.

## Planned Topics

- **Thoracic Spine Mobility:** Thoracic extension and rotation to achieve a clean streamline and overhead reach without compensating through the lumbar spine or anterior shoulder.
- **Shoulder & Scapular Mechanics:** Scapular upward rotation, posterior capsule health, serratus anterior activation, and internal/external rotation balance.
- **Ankle Plantarflexion:** Improving ankle flexibility for more propulsive freestyle and butterfly kicks without knee strain.
- **Hip Mobility & Pelvic Control:** Hip flexor length and glute activation to counteract desk posture and maintain a horizontal body line in the water.
