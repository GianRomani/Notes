## Training Context & Objectives

- **Primary Priority:** Injury-free training, joint longevity (especially shoulders and thoracic spine), and sustainable long-term wellness.
- **Secondary Objectives:** Rebuild swimming speed (drawing on a competitive and masters background) and gradually develop muscle mass and strength in the gym without rushing progression.
- **Target Weekly Split:**
  - **Swimming (3x / week):** Two structured team sessions (Zurileu) plus one self-organized weekend session (75–80 minutes, 3.5–4.0 km).
  - **Gym (2x / week):** Strength and conditioning sessions structured to complement water volume and manage cumulative fatigue.
- **Tracking Ecosystem:** Pixel Watch 2 and Google Health (future automated or semi-automated import into `Training/Logs/`).
