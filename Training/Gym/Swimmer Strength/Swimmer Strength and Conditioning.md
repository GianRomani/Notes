> **Status:** Coming soon — initial structure and study roadmap.

Applying dryland strength and conditioning principles to swimming—targeting force transfer in the water, muscle balance, and shoulder resilience.

## Planned Topics

- **Pulling Strength & Lat Development:** Pull-ups, lat pulldowns, cable pullovers, and horizontal rowing patterns that directly reinforce the swimming catch and pull phase.
- **Posterior Chain & Lower-Body Power:** Romanian deadlifts, hip thrusts, split squats, and trap-bar/goblet squats for stronger starts, push-offs, and dolphin kicks.
- **Shoulder Health & Antagonist Balance:** Balancing high-volume internal rotation from swimming with external rotation, scapular retraction, and controlled pressing (preventing swimmer's shoulder).
- **Core Stability & Force Transfer:** Anti-extension and anti-rotation work (dead bugs, Pallof presses, hanging leg raises) to connect upper- and lower-body propulsion.
- **Concurrent Training Interference:** Scheduling gym days relative to Zurileu and weekend swim sessions to preserve stroke quality and recovery.
