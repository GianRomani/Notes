Core principles of resistance training for a lifelong swimmer entering the gym for the first time, prioritizing safe technique, long-term joint health, and gradual hypertrophy and strength gains.

## Planned Topics

- **Hypertrophy vs. Maximal Strength vs. Power:** Rep ranges, load selection, tempo, and rest intervals, and how each adaptation supports long-term wellness and swimming performance.
- **Progressive Overload & Autoregulation:** Managing load, volume, and Reps in Reserve (RIR) / Rate of Perceived Exertion (RPE) to progress steadily without ego lifting or burnout.
- **Fundamental Movement Patterns:** Horizontal and vertical push/pull, hip hinge, squat, lunge, and anti-rotation core bracing.
- **Volume, Frequency & Recovery:** Structuring minimum effective dose training for 2 sessions per week alongside 3 weekly swimming sessions.
