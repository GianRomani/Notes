> **Status:** Coming soon — initial structure and study roadmap.

Overview of swimming biomechanics and technical principles, focused on translating long-standing intuitive water feel into explicit, repeatable mechanical cues.

## Planned Topics

- **Hydrodynamics & Drag Reduction:** Body alignment, head position, streamline mechanics, and minimizing frontal and wave drag.
- **Propulsion & Catch Mechanics:** Early Vertical Forearm (EVF), high-elbow catch geometry, hand pitch, and finish extension.
- **Core Connection & Body Roll:** Hip-driven vs. shoulder-driven rotation, timing between kick and pull, and maintaining a rigid kinetic chain.
- **Stroke-Specific Breakdowns:**
  - **Freestyle:** Breathing mechanics, stroke rate vs. stroke length (SWOLF), and pacing efficiency.
  - **Butterfly:** Undulation rhythm, two-beat kick timing, breath timing, and energy conservation.
- **Starts, Turns & Underwaters:** Flip turn mechanics, push-off streamline, and underwater dolphin kick efficiency.
