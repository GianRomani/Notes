#training/swimming #note

> **Status:** Coming soon — reusable weekend session library.

Collection of structured 75–80 minute (3.5–4.0 km) weekend swimming workouts designed for higher-intent group training.

## Planned Session Categories

- **Aerobic Threshold & CSS Sets:** Sustained pace work (e.g., broken 100s, 200s, and 400s on tight rest intervals) to rebuild aerobic engine and stroke efficiency.
- **Speed & Race-Pace Sets:** Short, high-output repeats (25s, 50s, and 100s) with generous rest to sharpen neuromuscular speed in freestyle and butterfly.
- **Individual Medley & Stroke Variety:** Mixed-stroke conditioning to build balanced shoulder loading and all-around feel for the water.
- **Technique & Active Recovery:** Lower-intensity drill-focused sessions for weeks with higher gym fatigue or travel.
