#training/swimming #note

> **Status:** Coming soon — initial structure and study roadmap.

Structured study of swimming physiology, energy systems, periodization, and workout design for a 3-session-per-week masters swimmer balancing team practices with a longer self-organized weekend session.

## Planned Topics

- **Energy Systems & Training Zones:** Aerobic base (EN1), threshold / Critical Swim Speed (EN2 / CSS), $\text{VO}_2\text{ max}$ (EN3), lactate tolerance (SP1/SP2), and alactic speed (SP3).
- **Critical Swim Speed (CSS):** Testing protocol (400m + 200m time trial), pacing calculations, and tracking aerobic progression over time.
- **Set Construction & Session Architecture:** Designing warm-up, pre-set, main set, and cool-down blocks for 75–80 minute (3.5–4.0 km) sessions.
- **Periodization on 3 Swims per Week:** Structuring macrocycles and mesocycles when combining two coach-led team practices (Zurileu) with one high-intent weekend session.
- **Recovery & Concurrent Load:** Balancing high-intensity water sessions with two weekly gym days to protect shoulder health and performance quality.
