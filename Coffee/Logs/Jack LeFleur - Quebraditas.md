**Jack LeFleur — Quebraditas** — Dial-in and festival tasting log for Colombian Orange Bourbon.

## Bean & Roaster Profile

- **Roaster:** [Jack LeFleur Specialty Coffee](https://jacklefleur.ch) (Zurich, Switzerland)
- **Coffee Name:** Quebraditas
- **Origin & Region:** Colombia
- **Varietal:** Orange Bourbon
- **Bag Notes:** Orange, citrus, grapefruit
- **Context:** Swiss Coffee Festival & Home Brew
- **Overall Rating:** ⭐ 8.5 / 10
- **Best Method & Dialed-In Setting:** Sage Espresso (step 2) & Festival Pour Over

## Dial-In & Brewing Trials

### Trial 1 — Swiss Coffee Festival (Pour Over)
- **Method:** Festival Pour Over (no home settings)
- **Tasting Notes:** Insane discovery at the Swiss Coffee Festival (**8.5/10**). Intensely vibrant and unmistakable orange/citrus notes.

### Trial 2 — Espresso (Sage)
- **Method:** Espresso (Sage) — [[Coffee/Recipes/Sage Espresso Base|Sage Espresso Base]]
- **Grinder & Setting:** Sage built-in grinder, step **2**
- **Tasting Notes:** Tricky to dial in on the Sage, but even so it pulled a very, very good shot (**8.5/10**) with prominent orange notes.

### Trial 3 — Milky Drink Experiment (Inspired by Matt Winton)
- **Method:** Espresso (Sage, step 2) + Steamed Oat Milk
- **Tasting Notes:** Attempted to replicate the [[Coffee/Logs/Rose x Commercial - Bar Takeover (Matt Winton)|Rose × Commercial Milky]] using oat milk at home. Ended up really well (**8.5/10**) — strong citrus notes carried through the milk nicely, with potential to be a 10/10 once paired with whole milk and improved microfoam technique.
