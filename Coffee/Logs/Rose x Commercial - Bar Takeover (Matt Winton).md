## Tasting Notes

- **Method:** Café Tasting (Competition Espresso + Milk Pairing)
- **Spro (9/10):** Insane and highly unusual espresso — zero crema (likely paper-filtered or filtered extraction style). Reminiscent of an AeroPress or Moka in clarity/texture, yet unmistakably preserving full espresso DNA.
- **Milky (10/10):** Best milk-based coffee ever tasted (tied with the *Tiger Bomb* in Melbourne the previous year). The berry notes cut right through the milk — which usually mutes delicate flavours — and merged with the milk texture seamlessly.
- **Follow-Up Inspiration:** Inspired a home milk-drink experiment with [[Coffee/Logs/Jack LeFleur - Quebraditas|Jack LeFleur — Quebraditas]] using oat milk (8.5/10).
