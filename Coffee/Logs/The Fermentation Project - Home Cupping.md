**The Fermentation Project — Side-by-Side Home Cupping** — Comparative cupping session with a friend following James Hoffmann's Fermentation Project protocol, tasting four identically sourced coffees processed four different ways across two roaster sets (**Zeff Coffee** + friend's roaster).

## Cupping Session Profile

- **Project:** [The Fermentation Project](https://www.thefermentationproject.com) — created by [James Hoffmann](https://www.youtube.com/@jameshoffmann) in collaboration with Lucia Solis and Caravela Coffee (4 variations of the same Guatemalan *San Miguel Urias* lot)
- **Roasters Compared:** [Zeff Coffee](https://zeffcoffee.com) (4 black jars: #1–#4) alongside a second roaster set (white pouches: #1–#4) brought by a friend
- **Context:** Home Cupping Session with a friend
- **Standouts:** Lots **#2** and **#3** (sweet & fruity profiles)

## Cupping Protocol & Setup

- **Method:** Blind / Side-by-Side Cupping (following James Hoffmann's Fermentation Project guide)
- **Equipment:** Timemore Chestnut C3S Pro, precision scale, cupping bowls & tasting glasses
- **Notes & Favorite Processes:** Lots **#2** and **#3** stood out as the favorites — noticeably sweeter and fruitier than the others. Worth exploring those specific fermentation processes further in future bags and [[Coffee/Theory/Coffee Cultivation & Processing|Coffee Cultivation & Processing]] study notes.

---

## Project Process Reference (San Miguel Urias, Guatemala)

1. **#1 — Mechanically Demucilaged (No Fermentation):** Control lot.
2. **#2 — Wild Fermentation (48h):** Traditional local ambient microbes.
3. **#3 — Lactobacillus Inoculated (48h):** Targeted bacterial fermentation enhancing organic acids and brightness.
4. **#4 — Yeast Inoculated (48h):** Specific yeast (*Saccharomyces* / Cima) building body and aromatic precursors.
