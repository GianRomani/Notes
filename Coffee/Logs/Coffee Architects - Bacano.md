**Coffee Architects — Bacano** — Dial-in and tasting log for Rodrigo Sanchez mango extended-fermentation Purple Caturra.

## Bean & Roaster Profile

- **Roaster:** [Coffee Architects](https://www.coffee-architects.com) (Thalwil/Zurich, Switzerland)
- **Coffee Name:** Bacano (250g)
- **Origin & Region:** Colombia — Huila
- **Producer / Farm:** Rodrigo Sanchez, Finca Monteblanco
- **Varietal:** Purple Caturra (Arabica)
- **Process:** Mango Extended Fermentation, Fully Washed
- **Altitude:** 1,730 masl
- **Roast Level:** Filter
- **Bag Notes:** Mango, elderflower, pineapple
- **Context:** Home Brew
- **Overall Rating:** ⭐ 9 / 10
- **Best Method & Dialed-In Setting:** Sage Espresso (step 2–3); Origami (C3S Pro: 10–15 clicks)

## Dial-In & Brewing Trials

### Trial 1 — Espresso (Sage)
- **Method:** Espresso (Sage) — [[Coffee/Recipes/Sage Espresso Base|Sage Espresso Base]]
- **Grinder & Setting:** Sage built-in grinder, step **2–3**
- **Tasting Notes:** One of the **favorite coffees of the year (9/10)**. Both the fragrance/aroma and cup flavor notes are incredible.

### Trial 2 — Origami Pour Over
- **Method:** Origami — [[Coffee/Recipes/Origami Pour Over|Origami Pour Over]]
- **Grinder & Setting:** Timemore Chestnut C3S Pro, **10–15 clicks**
- **Tasting Notes:** Slightly less expressive than on espresso, likely due to still refining pour-over technique.

### Trial 3 — Orange-Based Iced Mont Blanc
- **Method:** Signature Cold Drink (Iced Mont Blanc with orange juice, cream, vanilla, honey & orange zest)
- **Tasting Notes:** Solid **7/10** — fun experiment, though Bacano's mango/tropical profile may not be the ideal match for an orange Mont Blanc. Worth retrying with a different coffee base.
