The **Origami Dripper** is a versatile porcelain or resin brewer featuring 20 deep vertical ribs (flutes) that control air channels and bypass. By alternating between **flat-bottom (Kalita Wave)** and **conical (V60/Cafec)** paper filters, the Origami shifts between high-sweetness, round body brews and bright, high-clarity extractions.

---

## Equipment & Baseline Parameters

- **Brewer:** Origami Dripper (S or M)
- **Filter Options:**
  - **Conical Filter (V60 / Cafec Abaca):** High bypass air channels between flutes $\rightarrow$ faster flow, vibrant acidity, floral clarity.
  - **Flat-Bottom Wave Filter (Kalita 155 / 185):** Hugs the 20 ribs closely $\rightarrow$ restricted bypass, more uniform bed depth, enhanced sweetness and rounder body.
- **Grinder & Baseline Setting:**
  - **Timemore Chestnut C3S Pro:** 14–16 clicks (Conical filter: 13–15 clicks; Wave filter: 15–16 clicks)
- **Dose ($D$):** 15.0g
- **Water ($Y$):** 250.0g (Ratio 1:16.7)
- **Water Temperature:** 92°C–95°C on gooseneck kettle
- **Target Total Brew Time:** 2:30 – 3:00

---

## Step-by-Step Protocol (15g : 250g)

1. **Preparation:** Place filter (Conical or Wave) gently into the Origami grooves and rinse with hot water from the kettle to seal and preheat. Add **15.0g** ground coffee and level the bed.
2. **Bloom (0:00 – 0:40):** Pour **45g** water in a slow center-out spiral. Give a very gentle swirl (avoiding collapsing Wave filter pleats) and let bloom for 40 seconds.
3. **First Pour — Acidity & Fruit (0:40 – 1:10):** Pour from the center outward in tight concentric circles up to **150g**. Keep the kettle spout low to minimize excessive turbulence.
4. **Second Pour — Sweetness & Body (1:10 – 1:45):** Once the water level drops halfway, pour gently mostly in the center (coin-sized circle) up to **250g**.
5. **Drawdown (1:45 – 2:45):** Give a slight final wiggle to level the bed and allow complete drawdown.

---

## Filter Selection & Dial-In Guide

| Filter Type | Ideal Coffee Style | C3S Pro Grind | Cup Profile |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Conical (V60)** | Washed Ethiopians, Kenyans, Floral Geishas | **13–15 clicks** | Crisp acidity, fast flow, distinct fruit & floral separation. |
| **Flat Wave (Kalita)** | Naturals, Honey Process, Colombians, Sweet Mediums | **15–16 clicks** | Syrupy body, caramel/stone-fruit sweetness, forgiving extraction. |
