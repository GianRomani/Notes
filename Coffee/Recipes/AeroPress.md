The **AeroPress** combines full-immersion steeping with gentle air-pressure percolation through a paper micro-filter. Because immersion extraction is forgiving of flow channeling, the AeroPress produces a sweet, clean, and full-bodied cup across light to medium roasts.

---

## Equipment & Baseline Parameters

- **Brewer:** AeroPress (Standard Upright Position — safer and more consistent than inverted)
- **Filter:** 1 Paper Micro-Filter (no need to pre-rinse per Hoffmann blind tests, or quick rinse if preferred)
- **Grinder & Baseline Setting:**
  - **Timemore Chestnut C3S Pro:** **11 – 13 clicks** (Fine to Medium-Fine; 11–12 for light roasts, 13–14 for medium roasts)
- **Dose ($D$):** **11.0g** (Standard 200g cup) or **15.0g** (250g cup)
- **Water ($Y$):** **200.0g** (for 11g dose, Ratio 1:18) or **250.0g** (for 15g dose, Ratio 1:16.7)
- **Water Temperature (Kettle):**
  - **Light Roast:** 95°C–99°C
  - **Medium Roast:** 90°C–93°C
  - **Dark Roast:** 83°C–85°C
- **Target Total Brew Time:** **2:30 – 2:45**

---

## Step-by-Step Protocol (James Hoffmann Upright Method)

1. **Setup:** Insert 1 paper filter into the AeroPress cap, twist onto the chamber, and place upright on a sturdy mug or carafe over a scale.
2. **Dose:** Add **11.0g** (or **15.0g**) of freshly ground coffee and shake lightly to level the bed.
3. **Pour (0:00 – 0:15):** Start the timer and pour all **200g** (or **250g**) of hot water from the kettle, wetting all grounds during the pour.
4. **Insert Plunger Seal (0:15):** Immediately insert the plunger roughly **1 cm** into the top of the chamber and pull up slightly. This creates a vacuum seal that stops coffee from dripping through the filter during immersion.
5. **Steep (0:15 – 2:00):** Let the coffee steep undisturbed until **2:00**.
6. **Swirl & Settle (2:00 – 2:30):** Holding both the mug and AeroPress chamber, give the brewer a gentle swirl at **2:00** to knock down the floating crust. Wait **30 seconds** so all grounds settle into a flat filter bed at the bottom.
7. **Gentle Press (2:30 – 3:00):** Press down very gently and steadily (using just the weight of your forearm) for **30 seconds**, pushing all the way through the hiss.

---

## Concentrate / Iced / Milk Bypass Variant (18g : 90g)

- **Dose:** **18.0g** ground finer (**9–10 clicks** on C3S Pro).
- **Water:** **90.0g** at 92°C–95°C.
- **Protocol:** Pour 90g water, stir vigorously for 10 seconds with the paddle, insert plunger seal, steep until **1:00**, swirl gently, and press from **1:15 – 1:45**.
- **Serving:** Dilute with 90g–110g hot water (Americano style), pour over 100g ice (Japanese Iced AeroPress), or top with steamed/frothed milk.
