# Hoffmann V60 Technique
#coffee/recipe #note

## Resources
- [James Hoffmann - The Ultimate V60 Technique (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AI4ynXzkF50)

## Default Parameters
- **Ratio:** 60g/L (e.g., 15g coffee to 250g water)
- **Temperature:** 95°C+ for light roasts
- **Grind:** Medium-fine

## Steps
1. **Bloom:** Pour 3x weight of grounds (e.g., 45g). Swirl to wet all grounds. Let bloom for 45s.
2. **First Pour:** Pour to 60% of total volume (e.g., 150g) by 1:15 in circular motions.
3. **Second Pour:** Pour slowly up to 100% (e.g., 250g) by 1:40 in the center.
4. **Agitate:** Stir once clockwise and once counter-clockwise to clear walls.
5. **Swirl:** Give the dripper a gentle spin as it drains for a flat bed.
