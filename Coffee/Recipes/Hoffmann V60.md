The **James Hoffmann V60 Technique** (1-cup 5-pour and classic 2-pour variants) is a repeatable pour-over protocol designed to maximize thermal stability, extraction evenness, and sweetness in conical drippers.

---

## Equipment & Baseline Parameters

- **Brewer:** Hario V60 (01 or 02)
- **Filter:** Hario Bleached Paper Filter (rinsed with hot water)
- **Grinder & Baseline Setting:**
  - **Timemore Chestnut C3S Pro:** 13–15 clicks (Medium-Fine)
  - **Sage Built-in Grinder:** Filter range (if needed, though C3S Pro is preferred for unimodal clarity)
- **Dose ($D$):** 15.0g (1-cup) or 18.0g (250–300g cup)
- **Water ($Y$):** 250.0g (Ratio 1:16.7 / 60g/L)
- **Water Temperature:**
  - **Light Roast:** 95°C–99°C
  - **Light-Medium Roast:** 92°C–94°C
  - **Medium Roast:** 88°C–91°C
- **Target Total Brew Time:** 2:45 – 3:15

---

## Step-by-Step Protocol (15g : 250g)

### 1. Classic Two-Pour Protocol
1. **Rinse & Dose:** Rinse paper filter thoroughly to preheat brewer and remove papery taste. Add **15.0g** coffee and create a small indentation (divot) in the center of the bed.
2. **Bloom (0:00 – 0:45):** Pour **45g** water ($3\times$ dose) evenly over the grounds. Immediately swirl the dripper until all dry pockets are saturated. Let bloom until **0:45**.
3. **First Main Pour (0:45 – 1:15):** Pour steadily in concentric spirals up to **150g** (60% of total water weight) by **1:15**.
4. **Second Main Pour (1:15 – 1:45):** Pour gently to maintain water height up to **250g** (100% of total water weight) by **1:45**.
5. **Stir & Swirl (1:45):** Give 1 gentle stir clockwise and 1 counter-clockwise with a spoon to knock grounds off the paper walls, then give the V60 a gentle swirl to flatten the bed as it drains.
6. **Drawdown (1:45 – 3:00):** Allow complete drawdown. The finished coffee bed should be flat and uniform.

### 2. 1-Cup Five-Pour Variant (5 × 50g)
- **0:00 – 0:45:** Pour to **50g**, swirl gently, and wait until 0:45.
- **0:45 – 1:00:** Pour to **100g**, pause 10s.
- **1:10 – 1:20:** Pour to **150g**, pause 10s.
- **1:30 – 1:40:** Pour to **200g**, pause 10s.
- **1:50 – 2:00:** Pour to **250g**, swirl gently, and allow full drawdown by ~3:00.

---

## Dial-In Adjustments

- **Sour / Thin / Under-Extracted:** Grind 1 click finer on the **Chestnut C3S Pro** (e.g., 14 $\rightarrow$ 13 clicks) or increase kettle temperature by 2°C.
- **Bitter / Astringent / Slow Drawdown (>3:30):** Grind 1 click coarser on the **Chestnut C3S Pro** (e.g., 14 $\rightarrow$ 15 clicks) or reduce gentle swirl agitation.
